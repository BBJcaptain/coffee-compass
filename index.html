About Coffee Compass

Your preflight brief.

What this is

Coffee Compass is your flight bag for good coffee. Wherever crew are based or laid over, it points you to a short, hand picked list of proper coffee bars within walking distance of the crew hotel, so you spend your rest finding a quiet seat, not scrolling.

How to fly it

Start on the map, or pick your airport from the dropdown up top. Choose your hotel if there is more than one, since that is where you are walking from. Each coffee bar shows the walk time from your hotel, and when it is a longer walk, a bus and a train option too. You also get tags for what it offers, my airplane rating and a line on why it is worth the walk. Tap Directions for its Google profile, or Reviews for the crowd view.

Keep it in your flight bag

Install it to your home screen and it opens like any app, no store required. On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the three dot menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Done in seconds, ready before your next duty.

Who rates them

The airplanes are my mark, one to five, as a captain who has actually sat in these places with a coffee in hand. One expert opinion, not an anonymous crowd.

Want the crowd view, or to leave your own?

Every card links straight to the venue's Google reviews. Read the room there, and if you want to leave a comment of your own, that is the place to do it. Coffee Compass keeps no reviews of its own.

Security and privacy, kept simple

The app is a single file served over HTTPS, with a strict content security policy and no third party trackers. No login, no account, and no data collected about you, because there is nothing here to log in to. The full detail is under Privacy in the footer.

Found a gem, or a dud?

Hit Feedback in the footer. It opens a prefilled email to me. Tell me what to add, drop or fix, and I will keep the list sharp.

Sources

Map tiles by Esri, with OpenStreetMap contributors among the sources. Coffee listings are open information; venue links go to Google.

Privacy, the short pre flight briefing

Welcome aboard. Before you taxi off for a coffee, here is exactly what Coffee Compass does and does not do with your data. It is short, because there is not much to tell.

We do not know who you are

No account, no login, no sign up. You are an anonymous passenger and we would like to keep you that way.

We do not track you

No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking cookies following you down the jet bridge. Coffee Compass is not air traffic control and has no interest in watching your every move.

Tap through and you leave our cabin

The links to a venue's Google profile and reviews, the transit directions, and the map itself, come from Google, Esri and OpenStreetMap. The moment you load the map or follow a link, those third parties receive the ordinary technical details any website visit involves, such as your device and network address, under their own privacy policies and not ours.

Write to us and you have shared your email, only that

The Feedback button opens your own mail app, addressed to serge@dutchmountain.coffee. If you send it, we see your message and your email address, and we use them only to read and reply. Nothing more.

Installing the app changes none of this

Adding Coffee Compass to your home screen keeps everything above exactly the same. Still no account, still nothing collected.

That is the whole briefing. No hidden compartments, no fine print in the seat pocket. Enjoy the coffee, and mind the step on the way out.

Questions? Serge is at serge@dutchmountain.coffee.
